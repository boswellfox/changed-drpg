The main story of CHANGED DRPG is:

1. In the middle of an embrace with [Puro](Characters/Latex/Puro.md) in the Changed (2018) universe, [Colin](Characters/Human/Colin.md) is transferred to an alternative universe (CHANGED DRPG). The game manager can choose whether Colin keeps his past knowledge of the [Latex](Species/Latex.md).
1. Colin must now escape [the new Lab](Locations/The%20Lab.md) and reach the real world.
1. Upon reaching the real world, it is radically different than in the Changed (2018) universe. The Pale Virus has been cured without the Latex, and normal human life has continued. Colin can now continue in an optional Epilogue (which is not [The Assimilation Prologue](Concepts/The%20Assimilation%20Prologue.md)) where he must find out a way to bring the Latex creatures into society, or stop the game here.
