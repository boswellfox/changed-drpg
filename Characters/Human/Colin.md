**Colin** is the main protagonist and player character of CHANGED DRPG, Changed (2018), and Changed: Special Edition (2022).

### Battling

Colin can level up, but stat and EXP increases are GM-powered.

## Appearance

Colin appears to be a scrawny man with messy brown hair and bright blue eyes, wearing only latex shorts. _He is in his twenties._

## Behavior

Colin is a silent protagonist, and is controlled by the player.

> [!note] Note
> If there are multiple players playing CHANGED DRPG, each will play as their own human with a design of their choice, or they can play as a latex beast. If one player is present, they will automatically play Colin.

He has been in cryogenic stasis for so long, he can't remember how he ended up in the tower or who's been taking care of him.

> [!error]- **Spoilers hidden.** (Story content)
> Unlike in Changed (2018), Colin does not have the Pale Virus in CHANGED DRPG. In fact, he doesn't have any sort of virus at all.
