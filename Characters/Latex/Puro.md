**Puro** is a [[Dark Latex]] creature that [[Colin]] may encounter in CHANGED DRPG.

> [!error]- **Spoilers hidden.** (Story contents)
> Puro is the secondary antagonist.

## Appearance

Puro is a Dark [[Latex]] creature with dense, soft "fur", and a body similar to that of an anthropomorphic wolf. His face is adorned with a white mask -- as with most Dark Latex creatures -- with white pupils visible through the eyeholes (dissimilar to Changed (2018), his eyes glow and are visible in the dark). In contrast to other dark latex creatures, he is somewhat taller than them and also has a larger tail.

Colin can not [[Battle]] Puro.

## Behavior

Unlike the rest of his species -- whose behavior is more instinctual and animal-like -- Puro exhibits a notably higher sapience than most of the creatures that [[Colin]] will encounter. Puro is a curious and compassionate being.

> [!error]- **Spoilers hidden.** (Story content)
> Puro has read about humans for a long time, and Colin is the first human he will ever meet.
>
> If Puro turns against the human (from dialogue, continued violence, or other triggers) and manages to die, then the crystal his mask creates will summon [[PURO V2]].
