The **Hypno Cat** is an enemy that [Colin](../Human/Colin.md) can face in the Lab. She is a black and glowing green variegated-[Latex](../../Species/Latex.md) cat found in the vents (she is not Dark or Light Latex).

> \[!note\] For game managers
> You can stop Hypno Cat from showing at all, if you wanted to.

## Battle Statistics

| Name    | Amount |
| ------- | ------ |

### Battle

If Colin reaches zero Health, she will grab his head, forcing him to look at her hypnotic glowing patterns. She transfurs Colin while he is under this hypnosis.

Her only ACT is "Hypnotize", where she voids a human player's turn. She is not exactly powerful.

When healing from low Health during downtime, she accidentally triggers her hypnosis effect and enters a sort of trance to heal faster.

## Appearance

The Hypno Cat is a special Latex creature. She is a black cat the same size as [Colin](../Human/Colin.md). Her eyes, mouth, and stripes glow a sickly bright luminous green.

## Behavior

If the Hypno Cat spots Colin inside the vents, she will attempt to crawl over and start a [Battle](../../Concepts/Battle.md). In [The Assimilation Prologue](../../Concepts/The%20Assimilation%20Prologue.md), she is completely silent and will not converse with the player.

> \[!note\] Note
> The Hypno Cat can not hypnotize Colin to perform any actions, only to temporarily freeze him.

> \[!note\] **For game managers**
> The game manager can optionally make Hypno Cat tamable and become an ally in battle after (and if) the player SPAREs her, with her ACT instead freezing an enemy, skipping its turn.
