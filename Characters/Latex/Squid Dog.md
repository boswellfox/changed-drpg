The **Squid Dog** is a [[Light Latex]] anthropomorphic mix of a squid and a wolf. Unlike the [[Tiger Shark]], it is not based on a real-life animal.

### Battling

The Squid Dog has three ACTs. "Lob", which throws a [[Minor Latex Shark]] at an enemy character, "Roar", which stuns any Latex allies, skipping their turn, and "Wraparound", which wraps their tentacles around a human ally, dealing 10 Health and skipping their turn. The Squid Dog will never agree to a SPARE.

## Appearance

The Squid Dog is a large four-armed light latex squid-dog creature, having the facial features and fluffy body of a canine and the limbs of a squid that it uses in an attempt to transfur [[Colin]]. It appears with the Tiger Shark tied up in its grasp.

## Behavior

The Squid Dog, unlike some other bosses, was never a human. It instead originates from experimentation and hybridization of Light Latex creatures with aquatic animals, much like all of the other aquatic beasts.

The Tiger Shark could be considered the Squid Dog's "pet". In [[Battle]] (and in the [[The Assimilation Prologue]]), the Squid Dog will refuse to speak to Colin. It shares the same beliefs of humans as the Tiger Shark.
