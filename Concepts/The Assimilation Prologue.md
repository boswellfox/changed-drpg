**The Assimilation Prologue** (also known as the **ASSIMILATION PROLOGUE** in-game) is a prologue after all humans have been transfurred (or, Colin in a single-player game) where the humans can continue to play CHANGED DRPG as Latex creatures.

Because the humans are Latex creatures in the Assimilation Prologue, they can not complete the game. However, they can still explore the facility and talk with the Latex. The Assimilation Prologue can end at any time, and a new story can begin.

> [!note] Dice rolls
> A game manager can potentially lock the Assimilation Prologue behind a dice roll, since humans retaining consciousness with transfurmation is a rarity.
