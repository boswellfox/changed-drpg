A **Battle** (also known as a **BATTLE** or a **FIGHT** in-game) is, well, a battle.

## Combat

Combat is turn-based, similar to RPGs like Deltarune. First, all human players will take their turns, either ATTACKing, DEFENDing, ACTing, or SPARE-ing an enemy. Then, all enemies will take their turns (they can only ATTACK and DEFEND in most cases).

### Statistics

#### Power

**Power** is the character's strength. ATTACKing will subtract the exact number of health points from the target. _It can be infinite._

#### Defense

**Defense** dulls ATTACKs. It will lessen the power by that percent. _Example: 20 Power vs. 25 Defense = 15 damage._

#### Health

**Health** is, well, Health. _It can be infinite._

### ACTing

A character can ACT in any number of ways that the game manager permits. For example, [Hypno Cat](../Characters/Latex/Hypno%20Cat.md)'s only ACT is "Hypnotize", where she voids a human player's turn. A human player only has "Check" (displays the enemy character's stats) and "Talk" (GM-powered) by default, but more can be added by the game manager (and "Talk" can even be changed). When fighting a Dark Latex, all human players have the "Hit Mask" ability, which makes them attempt to hit the creature's mask.

### SPARE-ing

A character can SPARE the other if they have been encouraged to SPARE each other (similar to Deltarune). However, one can not SPARE if the other one is not willing.

### Critical Hits

There is a very small chance (rolling 1 in a D20 die) that an ATTACK will completely ignore defense and deal full damage.
