**Latex** is the substance that composes every Latex creature in CHANGED DRPG, and the cause of transfurmation.

## Properties

Latex is a living material that feels like goo, and moves on its own, attracted to humans. Contact with Latex initiates a transfur if the human reaches zero HP (like in [Battles](../Concepts/Battle.md)).

Speaking of Battles, guns and other piercing weapons have no effect on Latex. Instead, blunt weapons and displacement guns like shotgun rifles have more effect than standard attacks. For example, a shotgun rifle can deal 50 damage to a Latex enemy.

## Transfurmation

When a human reaches zero Health from Latex contact (Latex deals damage upon contact, see [Battle](../Concepts/Battle.md)), they transfur, transforming their bodies into one of the Latex that transfurred them. Normally, they lose their consciousness and ability to make independent actions, but they retain them in [The Assimilation Prologue](../Concepts/The%20Assimilation%20Prologue.md).
